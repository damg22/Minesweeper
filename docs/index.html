First launch

The apps aren't signed with a paid certificate, so your computer asks once before opening them.

Windows

  1. Open Minesweeper-windows.exe.
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.

Mac

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Right-click Minesweeper.app → Open → Open.
  3. Still blocked? System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

How to play

Open every cell without a mine and flag all the mines. Each number tells you how many mines touch that cell, diagonals included.

Left clickOpen a cell
Right click or Ctrl/⌘ + clickPlace or remove a flag
Smiley, F2 or NNew game
1 2 3Beginner / Intermediate / Expert
EscQuit

Features

Three classic sizes

Beginner 9×9, Intermediate 16×16 and Expert 30×16, plus custom boards from the command line.

Safe first click

Mines are placed after your first click, so you never lose on move one.

The classic look

Bevelled tiles, red LED counters and a smiley that reacts to every click.

Play anywhere

Right here in your browser, or as a small download for Windows and Mac. No Python or other setup required.

For teachers

Use Minesweeper as a programming assignment. Students get this complete, working game with the rules removed, and write them back in. They see every change on screen, and automated tests tell them when each part is right.

What students practice

2D grids and bounds checks, flood fill as graph search, state machines, constrained randomness, and working from a spec and tests. Late CS1 or CS2, about 4–8 hours.

What you get

The finished UI, a game.py with empty methods and clear docstrings, a student handout, and 32 pytest tests in four milestones, ready for autograding.

  1. Setup: place mines (first click always safe) and count neighbours
  2. Reveal: open cells, lose on a mine, flood-fill empty areas
  3. Flags: flag and question-mark cycle, the mine counter
  4. Win and chord: detect a win, open around a satisfied number
